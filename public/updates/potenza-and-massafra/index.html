Standing with Potenza and Massafra

5 July 2026 · Pastor Mirko Ronchi

Dear friends,

In our last update we told you about Connect Church in Giugliano. Alongside that work, God has given us the privilege of standing beside two other churches, each with its own story and its own challenges: Potenza and Massafra. The work is theirs, and above all it is His. We are simply grateful to be part of the story.

Potenza

In Potenza, a small church was close to closing its doors. Then a group of young people chose to take responsibility for it. We were blessed to stand with them through the first and most delicate season, encouraging them and giving them the tools and training to lead the church.

We still walk beside them today. Leading a church while still young, in a city where the Gospel is little known, is not easy. Our prayer is that their ministry will flourish, and that many in their city will find a home in this church.

Massafra

In Massafra, the church lost its senior pastor to Covid in 2020. It was a painful time for the whole community. Today Francesco and Arianna Rotelli are carrying on a work with a long history, with the courage to look towards a new future.

It is hard and tiring work, and also glorious. It is an honor for us to encourage them along the way, as they care for the church and lead it into a new season.

How you can pray

  • For the young leaders of the church in Potenza: for wisdom, strength and unity.
  • For Francesco and Arianna and the church in Massafra.
  • For both churches to grow and to shine in their cities.

Thank you for walking with us, and with them.

Pastor Mirko
