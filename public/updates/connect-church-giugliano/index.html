A New Church in Giugliano

3 May 2026 · Pastor Mirko Ronchi

Dear friends,

One of the quiet joys of our church’s life has always been walking alongside other churches. Our own church was born in the 1970s through the work of Dutch missionaries, and we know how much it means when someone believes in you and stands behind you. Today we want to tell you about a church that was born from that same spirit.

A shared desire

Vincenzo and Numa had a burden for Giugliano, near Naples. After a season of getting to know each other, praying and working together, they told us their desire: to be part of the Pieno Vangelo family and to start the new church under our covering and guidance. For us it was both an honor and a responsibility, and we said yes with joy.

Thirteen baptisms

In 2021 Connect Church Giugliano was born. On that first day, thirteen people were baptized. It was a celebration of what God had already been doing in their lives, and a sign of what He was about to do in the city.

With our blessing

After the service, our pastoral team from Modugno, together with Pastors Thomas and George of Eleviva, a Canadian ministry that supports church planting in Italy, laid hands on Vincenzo and Numa and prayed for them. We blessed them as they began this new work in their own city and told them that they could count on us for any support they needed. It was a moving moment for all of us, and a clear start for the work in Giugliano.

Since 2021 we have watched the church grow and develop steadily, and today we keep walking alongside them in the new challenges they face, together with our friends at Eleviva.

How you can pray

  • For Vincenzo and Numa, and for their family.
  • For the people baptized that day and for all who are finding faith at Connect Church.
  • For the church to keep growing and reaching the city of Giugliano.

Thank you for walking with us, and with them.

Pastor Mirko
