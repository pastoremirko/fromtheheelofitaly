Welcome to From the Heel of Italy

18 November 2026 · Pastor Mirko

Dear friends,

Thank you for being here. Many of you have prayed for us, visited us or supported our work in Puglia over the years, and we have often wished we could keep you better informed. From the Heel of Italy is our answer to that.

Every two to four weeks we will send you a short update from one of the areas of ministry that grow out of our local church in Modugno:

  • Family Life – personal news from our home.
  • Our Church – Modugno – events, new projects and the vision we are pursuing for our city.
  • Il Rifugio Center – camps, retreats and conferences in Ruvo di Puglia.
  • To the Ends of the Earth – the workers and young people we prepare and send.
  • Plant & Strengthen – new churches in Puglia and support for churches that are struggling.

The email will be short. If you want to know more, the Read more link will bring you here, where each area collects longer stories, photos and videos.

Puglia, the heel of Italy, is still a region where many towns have no living church. We believe God wants to change that, and that from here the Gospel can reach the nations. Thank you for walking with us.

With gratitude,
Pastor Mirko
