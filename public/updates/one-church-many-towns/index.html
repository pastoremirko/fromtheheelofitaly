One Church, Many Towns

20 August 2026 · Pastor Mirko Ronchi

Dear friends,

When people think of our church, they think of Modugno, a town just outside Bari. But the believers who gather with us each week come from much further away.

A church spread across the province

The map shows where the believers of Pieno Vangelo come from. Modugno is at the center, but the dots reach across the whole province of Bari and beyond, from towns on the coast to towns far inland. God has given our church an impact that goes well past the borders of one town.

The towns circled in red are the places where we already have house groups that meet regularly. Week after week, believers open their homes to pray, read the Bible and care for one another, and to welcome friends and neighbors who don’t yet know Jesus.

A vision bigger than one town

Over the years, the Lord has given our church a wider vision and perspective: an impact that is not limited to Modugno, but reaches many other towns and cities. More and more, He is placing in our hearts the desire to plant new local communities in a strategic way.

This is the direction we are moving in. In the coming years we expect to open new campuses, and through them to reach new towns, welcome more families and see more people come to salvation. Our deepest desire is to see the Kingdom of God advance, and to see healthy, vigorous, visionary churches, with a real heart for the lost, planted in our province, in our region and beyond.

How you can pray

  • For our house groups, and for the families who lead and host them.
  • For wisdom as we discern where and when to open new campuses.
  • For the leaders God is raising up for these new communities.
  • That many people in these towns will come to know Jesus.

Thank you for walking with us.

Pastor Mirko
