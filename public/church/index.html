Church Life

Pieno Vangelo (Full Gospel) Church in Modugno, near Bari, was born in the 1970s through the work of Dutch missionaries. Here you'll find news on initiatives and the vision we are pursuing for our city and beyond.

1 November 2026

A Season of Growing Leaders

After years of big changes, God is raising up new leaders in our church and sharing the load in ways we had prayed for. We are grateful and full of hope.

Read more →Close ↑

Dear friends,

The last few years have brought big changes to our church. In prayer, the Lord kept showing us that after this summer a new season would begin: a season of change for our church and of growth in leadership. Now that autumn is here, we can see it happening.

Leaders who grow

What we are living now is a time of growing leaders. Men and women in our church are stepping forward, taking on responsibility and wanting to be on the front line, committed to the growth and multiplication of our church.

Leaders of leaders

A new role is taking shape among us: leaders who can embrace several areas at once. They support the local work and make sure that every leader receives care, guidance and equipping. They are also lightening the load that Giorgia and I have carried on our shoulders until now.

Grateful and hopeful

We are deeply grateful for this step. It is a joy to see growth in the way our church is led, and to see people giving themselves fully to this work. It gives us a hopeful view of the future: a church with more strength to multiply.

Our prayer is that, in God’s timing, new local churches will be born from our own.

How you can pray

  • For our leaders, both new and experienced: for wisdom, humility and joy as they serve.
  • For those stepping into the role of leading other leaders, that they may care for them well.
  • For unity in our church through this season of change.
  • For new churches to be born from ours, in God’s timing.

Thank you for walking with us.

Pastor Mirko
