Four New Workers, Sent from Our Church

Four believers from our church are heading to the unreached nations, one of them this very month. Here is their story, and why Italians are well placed to go.

Read more →Close ↑

Dear friends,

For years we have prayed that our church would send not only prayers and gifts to the nations, but people too. Today we are seeing that prayer answered.

Four new workers

Over the last two years, four believers from Pieno Vangelo (Full Gospel) Church in Modugno have heard God’s call to serve among unreached peoples. A young woman left for her field beyond our borders this very month. A young man will follow at the beginning of January, and a married couple, a little further along in life, will leave in early 2027.

We have watched them grow in faith, test their calling and prepare with patience. It is a privilege to see believers from our own church mature and step out like this, and we are glad to stand behind their decision.

A story that began in Puglia

Our workers serve with Frontiers Italia, and its Italian base is run from within our church. It grew out of i6:8, a work that began mobilizing young people here in Puglia in 2004, sent its first long-term workers in 2011 and joined Frontiers in 2014. Together, we prepare, send and care for workers among the peoples with the least access to the Gospel. You can learn more at frontiersitalia.org.

Why Italians?

An Italian passport opens doors. In many countries where the Gospel has hardly been heard, Italians can get a visa fairly easily compared with believers from many other parts of the world. We believe this is a strategic opportunity we shouldn’t underestimate: God has placed us in a country whose citizens can go where others often cannot.

How you can be part of it

If your heart is for the unreached nations to hear the Gospel, please consider supporting these workers with a monthly gift. You can give to Pieno Vangelo through our Support page: just write “Missions” as the reference. Every gift helps our workers stay on the field and do the work God has given them.

How you can pray

  • For the young woman already on the field: for strength, joy and good friendships as she adjusts to the many changes she is going through.
  • For the young man and the couple as they finish preparing and say their goodbyes.
  • For all four workers to receive the full financial support they need.
  • For more Italians to hear and answer God’s call to the nations.

Thank you for walking with us.

Pastor Mirko
