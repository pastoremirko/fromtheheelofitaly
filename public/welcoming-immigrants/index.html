Welcoming Immigrants to Christ

Many families come to Puglia from far away in search of a better life. We long to welcome them with the love of Christ, through friendship and practical help. Here you'll find stories of the people God is bringing to our doorstep.

24 November 2026

After School Is Back

Our after-school program for the children of immigrant families has started again, led by a missionary couple who came home with a heart for the nations living among us.

Read more →Close ↑

Dear friends,

Many families have come to Puglia from far away. In Modugno and the towns around it, we live side by side with people from all over the world, especially a large Indian Sikh community and many families from Muslim countries. Many are looking for a better life for their children, and many have never heard the Gospel.

A heart shaped on the field

A missionary couple from our church recently came home after serving for several years in Muslim countries. They returned with a special love and sensitivity for immigrants, and they soon saw that the nations they had gone out to serve are also here, on our doorstep. Today they are creating opportunities to get to know these families, to build friendships and to serve them, so that through these relationships we can share the Gospel.

After School is back

Through their service we have restarted our After School program. Many children of immigrant families struggle with Italian, and homework can feel like a wall. Our volunteers sit beside them, help them with their homework and give them a place where they are welcome. It is a simple way to love and serve these families and to build real relationships with them.

A story that starts again

This program is not new for us. Some years ago it reached more than twenty different families. Over time, though, we had fewer and fewer volunteers, until we had to stop. Today things look different. Thanks to donors who care about this work, and to many people ready to give their time, we believe the After School can now go forward with new strength.

We believe that many of these families can be reached in this way, and we are praying and hoping to see many of them come to Christ.

How you can pray

  • For the children and their families: that they may feel welcomed and loved.
  • For the couple leading the program, and for every volunteer who serves with them.
  • That many of these families will come to know Jesus.

If you would like to help keep After School going, you can find out how on our Support page.

Thank you for walking with us.

Pastor Mirko
