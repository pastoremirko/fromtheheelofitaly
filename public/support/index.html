Thank you for considering standing with us in prayer, visiting us and giving.

Pray with us

Prayer is the first and most important way to support us. Every update ends with a few short prayer points: we would be grateful if you made them part of your prayers.

Visit us

If you are traveling to Southern Italy, we would love to welcome you to our church in Modugno, near Bari, or to Il Rifugio in Ruvo di Puglia. Write to us and we will help you plan your visit.

Give

Gifts to our ministry are received through Adventive Cross Cultural Initiatives (ACCI), our partner organization.

  • From the United States (USD): give online at adventive.ca/make-a-donation-us.
  • In euros: give online at adventive.ca/make-a-donation-euro.

On the donation form, under Select fund, choose Europe, then Italy Pieno Vangelo.

Giving online is the simplest way. If you need to give by check, please make it payable to ACCI, add a note saying it is for Italy Pieno Vangelo, and do not write our names anywhere on the check.

Thank you for your generosity.
