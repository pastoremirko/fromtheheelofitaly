Loving the Nations Among Us

Many come to Puglia from far away, leaving behind hardship and danger in search of a better life for their families. We don't want them to feel like strangers here. We long to welcome them with open arms, to love them as Christ has loved us, and to serve them through friendship, practical help and our after-school program, so that they can find a home among us and come to know the love of Jesus.
