Standing with Potenza and Massafra

Alongside the new church in Giugliano, we walk beside two churches facing real challenges: Potenza and Massafra.

Read more →Close ↑

Dear friends,

In our last update we told you about Connect Church in Giugliano. Alongside that work, God has given us the privilege of standing beside two other churches, each with its own story and its own challenges: Potenza and Massafra. The work is theirs, and above all it is His. We are simply grateful to be part of the story.

Potenza

In Potenza, a small church was close to closing its doors. Then a group of young people chose to take responsibility for it. We were blessed to stand with them through the first and most delicate season, encouraging them and giving them the tools and training to lead the church.

We still walk beside them today. Leading a church while still young, in a city where the Gospel is little known, is not easy. Our prayer is that their ministry will flourish, and that many in their city will find a home in this church.

Massafra

In Massafra, the church lost its senior pastor to Covid in 2020. It was a painful time for the whole community. Today Francesco and Arianna Rotelli are carrying on a work with a long history, with the courage to look towards a new future.

It is hard and tiring work, and also glorious. It is an honor for us to encourage them along the way, as they care for the church and lead it into a new season.

How you can pray

  • For the young leaders of the church in Potenza: for wisdom, strength and unity.
  • For Francesco and Arianna and the church in Massafra.
  • For both churches to grow and to shine in their cities.

Thank you for walking with us, and with them.

Pastor Mirko

3 May 2026

A New Church in Giugliano

In 2021 Connect Church was born in Giugliano, near Naples, with thirteen people baptized on its very first day. This is the story of how it began.

Read more →Close ↑

Dear friends,

One of the quiet joys of our church’s life has always been walking alongside other churches. Our own church was born in the 1970s through the work of Dutch missionaries, and we know how much it means when someone believes in you and stands behind you. Today we want to tell you about a church that was born from that same spirit.

A shared desire

Vincenzo and Numa had a burden for Giugliano, near Naples. After a season of getting to know each other, praying and working together, they told us their desire: to be part of the Pieno Vangelo family and to start the new church under our covering and guidance. For us it was both an honor and a responsibility, and we said yes with joy.

Thirteen baptisms

In 2021 Connect Church Giugliano was born. On that first day, thirteen people were baptized. It was a celebration of what God had already been doing in their lives, and a sign of what He was about to do in the city.

With our blessing

After the service, our pastoral team from Modugno, together with Pastors Thomas and George of Eleviva, a Canadian ministry that supports church planting in Italy, laid hands on Vincenzo and Numa and prayed for them. We blessed them as they began this new work in their own city and told them that they could count on us for any support they needed. It was a moving moment for all of us, and a clear start for the work in Giugliano.

Since 2021 we have watched the church grow and develop steadily, and today we keep walking alongside them in the new challenges they face, together with our friends at Eleviva.

How you can pray

  • For Vincenzo and Numa, and for their family.
  • For the people baptized that day and for all who are finding faith at Connect Church.
  • For the church to keep growing and reaching the city of Giugliano.

Thank you for walking with us, and with them.

Pastor Mirko
