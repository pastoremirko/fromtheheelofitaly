Alongside Other Churches

Giugliano, Potenza and Massafra: three churches God has given us the privilege to encourage and to bless.

Read more →Close ↑

Dear friends,

One of the quiet joys of our church’s life has always been walking alongside other churches. Not because we have it all figured out, but because we know how much it means when someone believes in you. Our own church was born in the 1970s thanks to missionaries from the Netherlands and the Dutch churches who stood behind them.

In recent years God has given us the privilege of standing beside three churches in particular. The work is theirs, and above all it is His. We are simply grateful to be part of the story.

Giugliano

In Giugliano, near Naples, a new church has been born: Connect Church. It is always moving to see a church start from nothing, with a few people, a lot of faith and a great deal of courage. We are thankful to have been able to support it, together with our friends from Eleviva, a ministry from Canada.

Potenza

In Potenza, a small church was close to closing its doors. Then a group of young people chose to take responsibility for it. We were blessed to stand with them through the first and most delicate season, encouraging them and giving them the tools and training to lead the church. We still walk beside them today. Our prayer is that their ministry will flourish, and that many in their city will find a home in this church.

Massafra

In Massafra, the church lost its senior pastor to Covid in 2020. It was a painful time for the whole community. Today Francesco and Arianna Rotelli are carrying on a work with a long history, with the courage to look towards a new future. It is hard and tiring work, and also glorious. It is an honor for us to encourage them along the way.

How you can pray

  • For Connect Church in Giugliano and those who lead it.
  • For the young leaders of the church in Potenza.
  • For Francesco and Arianna and the church in Massafra.

Thank you for walking with us, and with them.

Pastor Mirko
