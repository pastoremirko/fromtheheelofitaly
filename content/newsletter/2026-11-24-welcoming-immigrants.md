WELCOMING IMMIGRANTS TO CHRIST

After School Is Back

Dear friends,

Every week, our church doors open again to a group of children with backpacks, notebooks and a lot of questions.

Our area is home to people from all over the world, especially a large Indian Sikh community and many families from Muslim countries. Their children go to Italian schools, but at home there is often no one who can help with homework in Italian. Years ago our After School served more than 20 of these families. Then, without enough volunteers, we had to stop.

Now a missionary couple has come back from the field and brought the After School back to life. Around our tables, children read, do math and practice Italian, and their parents find a place where they are welcome. It's simple work, but it builds trust, and through that trust these families get to know the love of Jesus.

This is possible because some of you have chosen to give. Thank you.

Please pray for the children, their families and our volunteers.
