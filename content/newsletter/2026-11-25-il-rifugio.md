IL RIFUGIO CENTER

Getting Ready for a New Kitchen

Dear friends,

At Il Rifugio Center in Ruvo di Puglia we're working on many improvements. One of the most important is just ahead: we're getting ready to completely renovate and rebuild the kitchen. It will be one of the biggest investments Il Rifugio has made in the last ten years.

For a long time our volunteers have worked in a kitchen that was thrown together and only just adequate. The furniture and appliances are old, and they make the work harder. We have already cleared the space to make room for the work ahead.

Right now we're carefully looking at every option. We want the work done excellently, and within the means God has given us. Our goal is a place that is better suited to welcome groups, retreats and conferences, where guests are served food prepared in clean, healthy rooms that meet health and hygiene regulations. Serving will also be simpler and less tiring for our volunteers, in a space that is heated in winter and cooled in summer.

Please pray with us for wisdom in the choices we're making, and for everyone this kitchen will serve.
