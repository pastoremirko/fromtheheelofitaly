FAMILY LIFE

A New Season at Home

Dear friends,

Our home is full and busy these days. Johanna is now three and a half and has just started preschool, and Amedeo is one and a half. A new season has begun for our family, with new rhythms that are not always easy. But God is giving us the grace to live it well, and both children are a real joy.

With Giorgia and me both deeply involved in ministry, time always seems too short. We are learning to set priorities, to protect quality time as a family and not to let even good work take over. We are also learning to manage our finances in this new situation, trusting the Lord to provide for what our family needs.

One of the gifts of this season is that we are not walking it alone. Our church is full of young couples with small children, and Johanna and Amedeo already have many friends in their church family.

Would you pray with us for peace in stressful moments, wisdom with our time, God's provision, and for Johanna and Amedeo as they grow? If you would like to stand with us in a practical way too, you can find out how on our Support page.
