A New Church in Giugliano

Dear friends,

One of the quiet joys of our church's life has always been walking alongside other churches. Today we want to tell you how one of them began.

Vincenzo and Numa had a burden for Giugliano, near Naples. After a season of praying and working together, they told us their desire: to be part of the Pieno Vangelo family and to start a new church under our covering and guidance.

In 2021 Connect Church Giugliano was born, and on that first day thirteen people were baptized. After the service our pastoral team laid hands on Vincenzo and Numa, blessed them and told them they could count on us for any support they needed.

Since then the church has kept growing and reaching more people, and we are glad to keep walking beside them.

Would you pray with us for Vincenzo and Numa, for the people baptized that day, and for the church to keep reaching the city?
