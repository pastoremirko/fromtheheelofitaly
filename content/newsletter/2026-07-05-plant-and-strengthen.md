PLANT & STRENGTHEN

Standing with Potenza and Massafra

Dear friends,

Alongside the new church in Giugliano, God has given us the privilege of standing beside two other churches, each with its own story and its own challenges.

In Potenza, a small church was close to closing its doors. A group of young people chose to take responsibility for it, and we were blessed to stand with them through the first and most delicate season, encouraging them and giving them the tools and training to lead the church. We still walk beside them today.

In Massafra, the church lost its senior pastor to Covid in 2020. Today Francesco and Arianna Rotelli are carrying on a work with a long history, with the courage to look towards a new future. It is hard and tiring work, and also glorious, and it is an honor for us to encourage them along the way.

Would you pray with us for the young leaders in Potenza, for Francesco and Arianna, and for both churches to shine in their cities?
