TO THE ENDS OF THE EARTH

Four New Workers, Sent from Our Church

Dear friends,

God is sending people from our own church to the nations.

Over the last two years, four believers from Pieno Vangelo (Full Gospel) Church in Modugno have answered the call to serve among unreached peoples. A young woman left for her field this very month. A young man will follow in early January, and a married couple will leave at the start of 2027. They serve with Frontiers Italia, whose Italian base is run from within our church.

We have watched them grow, test their calling and prepare with patience. Now we get to send them, and we are thrilled to be a church with a real part in taking the Gospel where it has hardly been heard.

And there's something we don't want to overlook: an Italian passport opens doors. In many unreached countries, Italians can get a visa more easily than believers from much of the world.

Would you pray for these four workers this week? Want to support them? Give to Pieno Vangelo with the reference "Missions".
