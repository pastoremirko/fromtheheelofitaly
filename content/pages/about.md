title: About us
intro: A local church in Southern Italy with its eyes on the nations.
---

Full Gospel Church in Modugno, near Bari, was born in the 1970s from a missionary work supported by churches in the Netherlands. From the very beginning we have carried a clear vision: to be a church that responds concretely to the Great Commission, a community that looks from Puglia to the ends of the earth.

Puglia, the "heel" of Italy, is still a region where many towns, even large ones, have no living, healthy church. We long to see the Kingdom of God advance in Southern Italy and, from here, reach the nations.

Out of our local church grow several areas of ministry: the life of the church in Modugno, the **Il Rifugio** centre in Ruvo di Puglia, the sending of workers and young people abroad, and the planting of new churches together with support for existing churches that are going through difficult times.

*From the Heel of Italy* is how we keep our friends and supporters informed and invite them to pray and walk with us.
