Thank you for being here. Many of you have prayed for us, visited us or supported our work in Puglia over the years, and we have often wished we could keep you better informed. These updates are our answer to that.

On this site we regularly share updates from the areas of ministry that grow out of our local church, Pieno Vangelo in Modugno:

- **Family Life** – personal news from our home.
- **Church Life** – initiatives and the vision we are pursuing for Modugno and beyond.
- **Welcoming Immigrants to Christ** – welcoming those who have come to Puglia from other nations, with the love and the Gospel of Jesus.
- **Plant & Strengthen** – planting new churches in Puglia and standing alongside those that need support.
- **To the Ends of the Earth** – the workers and young people we prepare and send.
- **Il Rifugio Center** – a place of welcome and rest in Ruvo di Puglia, serving churches and ministries.

About once a month, our newsletter brings one of these updates to your inbox. Each email is short: if you want to know more, the *Read more* link brings you here, where each area collects longer stories, photos and videos.

## Who we are

Pieno Vangelo (Full Gospel) Church in Modugno, near Bari, was born in the 1970s through the work of Dutch missionaries and the churches in the Netherlands that stood behind them. From the very beginning we have carried a clear vision: to be a church that responds concretely to the Great Commission, a community that looks from Puglia to the ends of the earth.

Puglia, the "heel" of Italy, is still a region where many towns, even large ones, have no living, healthy church. We believe God wants to change that. Puglia also stands at a strategic crossroads, close to North Africa, the Balkans and the Muslim nations of the Middle East, and we believe Italians have a key role in carrying the Gospel from here to the nations.

Thank you for walking with us.
