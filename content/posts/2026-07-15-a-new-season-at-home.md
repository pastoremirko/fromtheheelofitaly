Dear friends,

Our home is full and busy these days. Johanna is now three and a half, and Amedeo is one and a half. Both of them are a real joy, and we are deeply grateful for the life and the family the Lord has given us.

## A new rhythm

Johanna has just started preschool. It is a small step for her and a big change for all of us: new routines, new emotions and new dynamics at home that are not always simple. But God is giving us the grace to live this season well, one day at a time.

## Learning to choose

Giorgia and I are both deeply involved in the life of our church, and time always seems too short. In this season of many changes we are learning to set priorities, to protect quality time as a family, and not to let even good and meaningful work take over everything. We are also learning to manage our finances in this new situation, to live a balanced life, and to trust the Lord in prayer for what our family needs.

## Not alone

What we love most about this season is the gift of family, and we are not walking it alone. Our church is blessed with many young couples with small children, families going through the same joys and challenges as we are. Johanna and Amedeo already have many friends in their church family, and it makes us so happy to see them grow up surrounded by love.

## How you can pray

- For peace and strength in stressful moments and in our emotions.
- For wisdom in how we use our time.
- For God's provision for the needs of our family.
- For Johanna and Amedeo: their growth, their education and their protection in everything they live and do.

If you would like to stand with us in a practical way as well, you can find out how on our [Support](/support/) page.

Thank you for walking with us.

*Pastor Mirko*
