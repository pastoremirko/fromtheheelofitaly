Dear friends,

Our church in Modugno was born in the 1970s from the work of missionaries from the Netherlands, supported by Dutch churches. We have never forgotten it. Someone planted and watered so that a church could grow here. From the beginning we have felt the responsibility to do the same for others: to help new churches be born, and to stand beside churches that are going through a hard time.

This first update from **Plant & Strengthen** is a simple map of where this is already happening.

**Potenza.** In the main city of Basilicata we have been walking alongside a church that has gone through a deeply difficult season. Our role is not to take over, but to support, encourage and help it get back on its feet.

**Giugliano.** Near Naples, we have accompanied a couple who started a new church community. Planting a church is lonely work, and we want them to know that they are not alone.

**Massafra.** In the province of Taranto, we supported the church through a delicate transition to new pastors. Nearby, in Mottola, a church plant we had begun came to a halt for various reasons. But the new leadership in Massafra has opened its doors to the believers reached in Mottola, and they are now part of a renewed church.

**Southern Puglia.** We have also begun a new church planting project in the south of our own region.

## Looking ahead

Our desire is that in the next ten years new churches will be born all across Puglia. It is still a region where, even in the larger towns, there is often no living and healthy church. God has already given us leaders who are prepared and carry this vision. Today they serve while balancing work, family and ministry, and we are praying for the way to release them to serve full time.

## How you can pray

- For the churches in Potenza, Giugliano and Massafra, and for their leaders.
- For the new church planting project in southern Puglia.
- For the leaders God is preparing to plant new churches in our region.

In the coming updates we will tell you more about each of these places and the people behind them. Thank you for standing with us.

*Pastor Mirko*
