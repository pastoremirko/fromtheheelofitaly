Dear friends,

At Il Rifugio Center in Ruvo di Puglia we are working on many improvements. One of the most important is just ahead of us: we are getting ready to completely renovate and rebuild the kitchen. It will be one of the biggest investments Il Rifugio has made in the last ten years.

## Where we are starting from

For a long time our volunteers have worked in a kitchen that was thrown together and only just adequate. The furniture and appliances are old, and they have made the work harder for the people who serve so faithfully behind the scenes. We have now cleared the space to make room for the work ahead.

## What we hope for

The plan is to rebuild the rooms from the ground up, with new floors, new plumbing and wiring, new furniture and some new appliances. Right now we are carefully looking at every option. We want the work done excellently, and within the means God has given us.

Our desire is for Il Rifugio to be better suited to welcome groups, retreats and conferences. We want to host our guests well and serve them food prepared in clean, healthy rooms that meet health and hygiene regulations.

We are thinking just as much of our volunteers. A new kitchen will make their service simpler and less tiring, in a space that is heated in winter and cooled in summer.

## How you can pray

- For wisdom as we look at every option and make our choices.
- For the work to be done well, and within our means.
- For the volunteers who serve in the kitchen, and for every guest who will be served there.

Thank you for walking with us.

*Pastor Mirko*
