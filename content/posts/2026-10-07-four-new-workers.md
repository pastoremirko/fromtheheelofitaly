Dear friends,

For years we have prayed that our church would send not only prayers and gifts to the nations, but people too. Today we are seeing that prayer answered.

## Four new workers

Over the last two years, four believers from Pieno Vangelo (Full Gospel) Church in Modugno have heard God's call to serve among unreached peoples. A young woman left for her field beyond our borders this very month. A young man will follow at the beginning of January, and a married couple, a little further along in life, will leave in early 2027.

We have watched them grow in faith, test their calling and prepare with patience. It is a privilege to see believers from our own church mature and step out like this, and we are glad to stand behind their decision.

## A story that began in Puglia

Our workers serve with Frontiers Italia, and its Italian base is run from within our church. It grew out of i6:8, a work that began mobilizing young people here in Puglia in 2004, sent its first long-term workers in 2011 and joined Frontiers in 2014. Together, we prepare, send and care for workers among the peoples with the least access to the Gospel. You can learn more at [frontiersitalia.org](https://www.frontiersitalia.org/).

## Why Italians?

An Italian passport opens doors. In many countries where the Gospel has hardly been heard, Italians can get a visa fairly easily compared with believers from many other parts of the world. We believe this is a strategic opportunity we shouldn't underestimate: God has placed us in a country whose citizens can go where others often cannot.
