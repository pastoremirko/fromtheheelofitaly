Il Rifugio was born from a simple desire: to create a place of welcome. A place where guests can step away from the rush of daily life and take time to rest, so that mind, body and spirit can be renewed.

We want it to be a place of **refreshment** at the service of churches and ministries. A place where workers for the harvest are **trained, prepared and helped to grow**. A place where the Church is **built up**, through camps, conferences, retreats and time spent together in God's presence.

Our prayer is that everyone who comes through its gates goes home strengthened, back to their church and their calling with new strength.
