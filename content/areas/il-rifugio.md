Il Rifugio is a Christian retreat centre in Ruvo di Puglia, in the countryside inland from Bari. Over the years it has become a home for the churches of Puglia and of all Italy: a place where people step away from daily life to meet God and one another.

Here we host **camps** for children, young people and families, **conferences** and **concerts**, **spiritual retreats** and **training courses** for leaders and people preparing for ministry. Il Rifugio is also a place of rest for **missionaries** passing through or recovering after years of service.

Its size and its location make it possible to hold events that no single local church could host on its own. Our desire is to keep improving it, so that it can serve the Kingdom of God for many years to come.
