Il Rifugio is a place of **refreshment** at the service of churches and ministries. Here guests find welcome and time to rest, and are renewed in mind, body and spirit.

It is a place where workers for the harvest are **trained, prepared and helped to grow**, and where the Church is **built up** through camps, conferences, retreats and time spent together in God's presence.

Those who come through its gates go home refreshed and strengthened, ready to return to their church and their calling.
