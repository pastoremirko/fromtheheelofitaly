# From the Heel of Italy – Style guide

One voice for the whole blog and every newsletter, so that every update sounds like it comes from the same family and the same church.

## Voice

- **Warm and personal.** We write as "we" (the church and the Ronchi family) to "you" (friends and supporters). Updates open with *Dear friends,*.
- **Simple and concrete.** Use short sentences and everyday words. Name real places, people and moments instead of general statements.
- **Grateful, not promotional.** God is the main actor and the people we serve are at the center. We are grateful to be part of the story; we don't boast or sell.
- **Hopeful and honest.** We can name difficulties (a closing church, a hard season) without drama, and always point to what God is doing.
- **Faith language that newcomers understand.** Talk about Jesus, prayer, the Gospel and the Church naturally, and avoid insider jargon.

## Language

- **English** for the whole site and the newsletter, with **American spelling** (*center, program, honor, traveling*).
- First mention of the church: **Pieno Vangelo (Full Gospel) Church in Modugno**; after that, *our church* or *Pieno Vangelo*.
- Names to write the same way every time: **Il Rifugio** (*Il Rifugio Center* in titles), **Ruvo di Puglia**, **Modugno, near Bari**, **Puglia** (not "Apulia"), **Pastor Mirko**, **the Ronchi family**.
- Area names, always the same: *Family Life · Church Life · Welcoming Immigrants to Christ · Plant & Strengthen · To the Ends of the Earth · Il Rifugio Center*.
- Missions: never name the exact countries or cities where our workers serve. Use regions ("North Africa", "Central Asia") or "beyond our borders".

## Area intros (site)

Two or three sentences: what the area is, why it matters to us, then **"Here you'll find…"** to say what the updates will contain.

## Newsletter email (the short piece)

- **Up to about 200 words**, one area per email.
- One photo, one story, then a **Read more** link to the full update on the site.
- Shape: a strong opening line → the heart of the story in 2–3 short paragraphs → one line inviting prayer → *Read more*.

## Full update (site article)

- **300–400 words**, written for one area (`area:` in the front matter).
- Opens with *Dear friends,*.
- One short paragraph of context, then the story. Use `##` sub-headings when there are several places or people.
- Ends with **## How you can pray** (2–4 short bullet points), then *Thank you for walking with us.* and the signature *Pastor Mirko* in italics.
- Every update needs a title (short, warm, no clickbait), an **excerpt** of one or two sentences and a cover photo with `cover_alt`.

## Photos

- Real photos of our people and places whenever possible. No faces of children without their parents' consent, and no faces or places that could identify workers in sensitive countries.
- Landscape format, at least 1200 px wide.
