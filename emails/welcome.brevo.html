Welcome

Updates from the Heel of Italy

Dear friend,

We're so glad you're here, and thrilled that you've chosen to walk with us! With this newsletter we'd love to keep you updated on what God is doing here in Puglia, the "heel" of Italy, and from here to the ends of the earth.

On our blog, hoi.place, you can follow every update on our ministry in this part of Italy. There you'll find news from these six areas:

Family Life

Our home, our children and how God is at work in our everyday life.

Church Life

Our church family in Modugno: its people, its growth and the vision for our city.

Welcoming Immigrants to Christ

Welcoming those who have come to Puglia from far away with friendship, practical help and the Gospel of Jesus.

Plant & Strengthen

Planting new churches in southern Italy and beyond, and walking alongside churches that need support.

To the Ends of the Earth

Training and sending workers beyond our borders.

Il Rifugio Center

Our center in Ruvo di Puglia, a place of rest and training for churches and ministries.

This story is still being written, and now you are part of it too. Every prayer, every word of encouragement and every gift helps this work grow stronger. Thank you for being with us!

Once a month, our newsletter will bring you an update from one of these areas, right to your inbox.
